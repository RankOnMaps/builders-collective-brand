05 · If You Get Stuck

when canva misbehaves.

Two known import quirks and how to fix them.
Quirk 01
Fonts look wrong after import.
Canva imports the PDF with embedded fonts, but if it can't read DM Sans it falls back to a default. Fix: select all text on the page (Ctrl+A) and re-apply DM Sans from the font dropdown. The Brand Kit makes this a one-click.
Quirk 02
REIWA map pages look pixelated.
The REIWA maps on pages 10-11 are high-res but Canva down-samples on import. Don't try to edit them in Canva. Either keep those pages as-is, or delete them in Canva and re-insert the original PNGs from the asset links below.
