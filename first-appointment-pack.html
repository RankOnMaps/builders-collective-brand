Page 05 · Reset
five things buyers
get wrong.
01
"going direct to a builder is cheaper."
Going direct is retail. We access the same builders at wholesale through volume agreements. Most clients save $25K to $45K on the same build.
02
"you'll just push me toward one builder."
We compare four builders on the same brief, same budget, same suburb. You see every quote side by side. You pick.
03
"i can handle this myself, it's not that hard."
Hidden costs, contract traps, and prestart upsells catch most first-time buyers off guard. Average overspend going solo: $18K to $30K against the original quote.
04
"using a broker makes everything more complicated."
One point of contact from finance to keys. We coordinate the trades, the certifier, the bank. You get one phone number, one inbox.
05
"there must be a fee on top of the build."
There isn't. We're paid a fixed fee by the builder, the same way an in-house display village consultant is paid. The price you see is the price you pay.
